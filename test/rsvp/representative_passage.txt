Morning light crossed the quiet room as a reader opened a small book. Outside the window birds moved between green branches and the road slowly filled with people. Each word arrived alone yet the story continued to grow in the mind. A steady rhythm made the simple passage easy to follow. Sometimes a longer word asked for more attention but this test kept every interval equal. The screen showed one word then another without leaving any word behind. At the end the reader paused looked back and wondered how quickly this little display could carry the next story into view.
